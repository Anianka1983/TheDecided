Hard enough for anyone. Harder when you're childfree.

Meeting someone who fits is difficult for everybody. The working hours are long, the circles narrow with age, and the people you would actually choose are rarely the people you happen to be introduced to.

Being childfree narrows it further. The pool is smaller, and a good deal of it wants something you do not. Shared values matter more here than in almost any other kind of match, and they are not something you want to leave to chance.

This service exists for people who chose this life and would like to share it with someone who has chosen the same. Nothing to justify and nothing to debate. Just someone who already understands, because they arrived at it themselves.

Things you will never hear from us

  • You'll change your mind
  • It's different when they're your own
  • You'll regret it
  • Who'll look after you when you're old?
  • Isn't that a bit selfish?

You've heard all of these. We're not going to be one more person asking you to justify something you settled years ago, and neither is anyone we introduce you to.

Who can apply

Childfree

Accepted on the strength of the interview

You made a settled decision not to have children and it has held up. We'll ask when you decided, what has happened since, and what you'd do if a partner changed their mind in two years. We never ask about anything medical, and if you mention it, we don't write it down.

Childless

Welcome to apply, discussed at interview

Some of our members wanted children once and aren't looking for that life now. You're welcome here, and we won't ask you what happened. What we will ask is where you are today: whether this is settled, or whether it's still something you're working through. Both answers are respectable. Only one of them is a fit for this service, and it's better for everyone that we establish which before you pay rather than after.

Parents

Not eligible, including adult children

Membership is limited to people who don't have children of their own. That includes adult children and children who don't live with you. We know this is stricter than some people would choose, and that plenty of childfree people date parents of grown-up children very happily. We hold the line because the whole value of a screened pool is that nobody ever has to ask, and one exception ends that. If this rules you out, we're sorry to say so, and we'd rather say it here than take your money first.

Four steps, one of which is us saying no

One

Join free

A short written application, about fifteen minutes. You tell us where you stand, how long you've stood there, and what your life looks like now.

Two

Interview

Forty-five minutes on video with your matchmaker. This is where a settled position separates from an open one, and it's the reason membership costs what it does. We ask about decision history, about how it has held up under pressure, and about the choices you've actually made around it. Nothing medical is asked, recorded or kept.

Three

Decision

We accept or we decline, and you hear which within five working days. We decline a meaningful proportion of applicants, most often because the position is genuinely still open. That isn't a judgement about anyone. It's the promise we've already made to every member in the pool.

Four

Introductions

One person at a time, with a written note explaining why this particular person and what you should know before you meet. Afterwards we ask you both what worked and what didn't, and the next introduction is better for it.

An algorithm has no reason to ask a difficult question

An app is paid for the size of its pool. Every undecided person it removes is a person it can no longer show you, so it never removes them, and the box you ticked three years ago has not been looked at since.

A matchmaker is paid for one introduction working. That's the whole of the difference. It means we can afford to spend an hour finding out whether someone means it, and it means we can afford to lose the ones who don't.

Questions people ask before applying

I'm childless rather than childfree. Is this actually for me?

It can be. The service is built around one thing: everyone in the pool is settled on not having children, and a person has checked. How you arrived there matters less than whether you've arrived. What we'd want to establish at interview is that this is a resolved position rather than a live question, because a member who is still working it out won't be happy here and won't make anyone else happy either.

Will you ask me why I don't have children?

No. We will never ask about fertility, sterilisation, contraception, treatment or any medical matter, and if you mention something of that kind we won't record it. Under UK data protection law that is special category health data and we have no business holding it. Every question we ask is about where you are now and what you want next, which is a better test in any case.

Can I join if I have adult children who've left home?

No. Membership is limited to people who don't have children of their own, and that includes adult and non-resident children. It's a stricter rule than some people would choose, and we know plenty of childfree people date parents of grown-up children very happily. We hold it because the value of a screened pool is that the question never has to be asked, and one exception ends that.

Won't mixing childfree and childless members cause problems?

It could, if it were done quietly. So we don't do it quietly. Every member knows both routes in before they join, every introduction states clearly which one applies, and nobody is ever put in front of someone whose situation they've told us doesn't work for them. Your matching preferences are yours and we don't argue with them.

How many members do you have?

We publish the figure by age band and region on our membership page and update it monthly. If the number in your band is too small to be useful to you, we'll say so before you pay rather than after.

Can you guarantee I'll meet someone?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. What we commit to is the process: a set number of introductions within a set period, each with a written rationale, plus the right to pause your membership.

What if I meet someone independently while I'm a member?

Tell us and we'll pause your membership on the spot, for up to twelve months. If it doesn't work out, you pick up the time you had left.

Is this discreet?

Yes. No public profiles, no browsable directory, no photographs shared without your say-so, and nothing about your membership visible to anyone outside the introductions we make.
