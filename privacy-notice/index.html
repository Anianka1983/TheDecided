1. Who we are

The Decided is a childfree matchmaking and introductions service operated by Sparked Connection Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 16967307. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice.

Contact: hello@sparkedconnection.com

Sparked Connection Ltd also operates sparkedconnection.com and singles-network.com. Applications made through The Decided are held in the same secure system, tagged to this brand. They are not merged into the other services, and you are not added to any other membership by applying here.

2. Our approach

Matchmaking only works if people tell the truth about themselves, and people only do that if they trust where it goes. We collect what we need to assess an application and make a considered introduction. Nothing more.

ImportantWe are not a dating app. Your profile is never publicly browsable, never searchable, and never shown to anyone except a specific person we are proposing to introduce you to.

3. What we collect

When you apply, we collect:

  • Name, email address and telephone number
  • Date of birth, and the age calculated from it
  • Town or city, and how far you would travel
  • Gender, and who you would like to be introduced to
  • Relationship status and relationship goals
  • Your answers about your decision not to have children, including when it settled and what has tested it
  • Occupation, profession level, industry, education, working pattern, and any business name, website or LinkedIn profile you give us
  • Living situation, height, build, hair colour and length
  • Languages, interests, values, habits around alcohol, smoking, exercise and diet
  • Optional personality test results (Myers-Briggs, love languages, attachment style)
  • Optional salary band
  • Photographs, where you upload them
  • How you heard about us
  • Any correspondence between us

5. What we deliberately never ask

Our service is built around whether someone has settled on not having children. The obvious way to test that would be to ask about sterilisation, contraception, fertility or medical history. We do not ask any of those things, and we never will.

That is health data. It is special category data of the most sensitive kind, we have no legitimate need to hold it, and holding it would create a risk to you that no benefit to us could justify.

If you mention it anywayIf you volunteer medical information in a free-text answer or during your interview, it is not recorded in your profile, not entered into our system, and not used in matching. Where it has already been captured in free text, we remove it.

Instead we establish the same thing by asking about decision history, how the decision has held up under pressure, and the practical choices you have made around it. That is a better test, and it costs you nothing.

6. How we use your information

  • To assess your application and decide whether to accept it
  • To prepare for and conduct your interview

  • To gather feedback after an introduction and improve the next one
  • To administer your membership, including payments and pauses
  • To contact you about your membership, and, if you have opted in, about events and the journal
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

7. Lawful basis

  • Contract — to provide the membership you have applied for or purchased
  • Legitimate interests — to assess applications, run the service responsibly, and keep our members safe
  • Explicit consent — for special category data, and for optional marketing emails
  • Legal obligation — for accounting and regulatory records

You may withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis we rely on.

8. How we share information

We do not sell your data, and we never will.

With a proposed match

Where we are proposing an introduction, we share a limited profile: first name or initial, general location, profession or lifestyle overview, high-level preferences, and selected photographs. Contact details are shared only after both people have agreed to meet.

With other agencies

Where you have told us you are open to it, we may discuss an anonymised outline of what you are looking for with other UK matchmaking agencies we work alongside. We do not share your name, photographs or contact details with another agency without asking you first.

With service providers

See section 9.

9. Who processes your data for us

Our application form submits to several services at once, so that no application is ever lost:

  • Our own client system — hosted on Vercel, with data stored in Supabase
  • FormBold, Formspree and FormSubmit — form delivery and email notification
  • Google (Sheets and Workspace) — a backup log and our email
  • Cloudflare — Turnstile, which confirms a human submitted the form and protects it from automated abuse
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for this website
  • Google Fonts — typefaces used on this site

Where a provider processes data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

10. AI-assisted tools

We use secure AI-assisted tools to help summarise questionnaire responses, draft internal notes, and identify possible compatibility patterns.

Human decisions onlyNo decision about your application, your membership or an introduction is ever made by automated means. Every acceptance, decline and introduction is made by a human matchmaker. AI supports that judgement; it does not replace it.

11. How long we keep it

  • Members — for the duration of membership and a reasonable period afterwards
  • Declined or withdrawn applications — kept briefly so we can explain a decision if asked, then deleted
  • Photographs — deleted on request at any time
  • Accounting records — as required by law

12. Security
