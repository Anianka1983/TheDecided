1. About us

The Decided is operated by Sparked Connection Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 16967307, which is registered with the Association of British Introduction Agencies.

By submitting an application or purchasing a membership, you agree to these Terms.

2. Nature of the service

We are a human-led introductions service for adults in the UK who are settled on not having children. Every applicant is interviewed by a person, and introductions are made by a matchmaker.

ImportantWe are not a dating app. There is no browsing, no swiping and no public profile. Introductions are proposed individually, following human review.

3. Eligibility and acceptance

By applying you represent, on an ongoing basis, that:

  • You are at least 18 years of age. Our membership is aimed at people between 28 and 50; applications outside that band are welcome but we may not have suitable people to introduce you to
  • You do not have children of your own, including adult children and children who do not live with you
  • You are settled on not having children, and this is not a question you are still working through
  • You are not currently married, in a civil partnership, or in an ongoing romantic or intimate relationship
  • Everything you tell us is true, accurate and complete, and you will tell us promptly if it changes
  • You are not subject to any court order, restriction or circumstance that would make your participation unsafe for other members

All applications are reviewed. Submitting an application or making a payment does not guarantee acceptance. We may decline, suspend or discontinue a membership where we reasonably consider it appropriate, including where participation may present a safety, legal or reputational risk.

4. Definitions

"Introduction" means a proposed meeting where both people have been told about each other and have agreed to be introduced.

"Membership period" means the six or twelve months beginning on the date your paid membership starts.

"The Register" means the pool of interviewed and accepted people who are considered for introductions but for whom no active search is carried out.

6. Number of introductions

The figures above are upper limits over the membership period, not a minimum and not a promise. Introductions depend on the availability of suitable people, mutual willingness to be introduced, and your own responsiveness and availability.

Introductions are not evenly spaced and may occur at any point during the membership period. Unused introductions do not entitle you to a refund.

7. No guaranteed outcomes

We do not guarantee that you will meet a partner, that any introduction will lead to a relationship, or that introductions will occur at a particular time or frequency. Introductions are made using professional judgement on the basis of the information available to us.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate, truthful and current information, and tell us if it changes
  • Engage respectfully with the people we introduce you to
  • Remain reasonably responsive to our communications
  • Tell us promptly, in writing, if you enter a relationship

Where you become unresponsive for more than 90 consecutive days without an agreed pause, we may pause your membership. Periods of non-engagement may not count toward your membership period.

9. Pausing your membership

You may ask us to pause your membership, for example if you meet someone independently, or for illness, bereavement, relocation or work. Requests must be in writing and are subject to our agreement.

Introductions stop during a pause and the paused time does not count toward your membership period. Introductions tiers allow one pause of up to twelve months; Dedicated Search allows two.

10. Behaviour and conduct

We may suspend or end a membership immediately where someone provides false or misleading information, breaches an eligibility representation, behaves in a way that is unsafe, dishonest, disrespectful or abusive, or misuses an introduction. In those circumstances fees are not refunded.

If you arrange to meet someone through us, you do so at your own risk. Take the same sensible precautions you would when meeting anyone you do not know.

13. Privacy and confidentiality

14. Profile sharing
